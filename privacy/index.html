Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bean Hunt collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Bean Hunt mobile app, website, and related services.

Who We Are

Bean Hunt is operated by Randy Ventures. Questions about this policy can be sent to randyventures06@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address, username, city, and securely hashed password credentials.
  • Content you submit, including coffee logs, ratings, comments, saved items, and uploaded photos.
  • Purchase and entitlement information for lifetime Premium, legacy subscriptions, or optional tips processed by Apple or Google.
  • Session, security, and technical data required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Location data you provide directly or allow from your device to support nearby discovery.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure user accounts.
  • To provide nearby coffee discovery, search, saved items, drink logs, and account personalization.
  • To store and display the content you choose to submit inside the app.
  • To send password reset or email verification messages.
  • To operate Premium purchases, legacy subscriptions, optional tips, and restore entitlements.
  • To detect abuse, moderate content, prevent fraud, and maintain service performance.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run Bean Hunt.

  • Infrastructure and database providers that host the API and account data.
  • Cloud storage providers that store uploaded photos and related media.
  • Email delivery providers used for transactional account emails.
  • Places and mapping providers used to return nearby coffee shops and shop details.
  • Apple and Google billing systems for in-app purchases and subscriptions, and RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts our public website and the catalog and artwork for owned-app promotions.

Photos, Reviews, and User Content

When you upload a photo or submit a review, rating, or journal entry, that content may appear in the app according to the product’s sharing behavior. Uploaded media is stored in private object storage and served through app-controlled access.

Location Data

Bean Hunt can use your device location to find nearby coffee shops. You may also search by city, neighborhood, or zip code instead. We do not use location for third-party advertising.

Premium and Purchase Support

Apple or Google processes in-app payments. We do not receive your payment-card number or store-account password. RevenueCat processes purchase, transaction, entitlement, and related technical information, using a generated purchase identifier or your Bean Hunt account identifier when signed in, to provide purchases, restoration, diagnostics, and purchase support. We do not use purchase records to target ads. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy for its practices.

Recommendations for Our Other Apps

Eligible users of the free version may see small, clearly labeled advertisements for other apps by Randy Ventures. Premium removes these advertisements, including for existing lifetime owners and active legacy Premium subscribers. We do not select ads based on your coffee entries, location, saved places, purchase history, or a personal advertising profile. We do not use an advertising identifier, installed-app inventory, or third-party advertising SDK for these promotions.

The app downloads a shared catalog and images over HTTPS from randyventures.com, hosted on GitHub Pages. The host processes ordinary network request information, including IP address, requested path, time, and connection information, to deliver and protect the service. Its retention is governed by the hosting provider’s practices; we do not receive a per-person ad-view or click event feed from the app. See the GitHub privacy statement. The app accepts catalog data and images, not executable ad code. Promotions are suppressed offline or when a valid catalog or purchase status is unavailable.

Promotion selection uses the available catalog, age-suitability checks, local rotation, and apps you have hidden. The last display time, session timing, and hidden-app choices stay on your device to exclude the first session, limit frequency, and respect your preferences. These preferences are not uploaded to an advertising service. You can dismiss a card or hide the promoted app without opening its store page.

If you choose to visit an advertised app, its App Store link includes a shared provider token and campaign name identifying the app and placement. These tags contain no identifier unique to you or your device. Apple may provide aggregate campaign reports under its own reporting rules. We do not join those reports to your Bean Hunt account or RevenueCat purchase record. Clicking, downloading, rating, reviewing, or purchasing another app is never required to continue using Bean Hunt.

If you choose Report ad, Bean Hunt opens an email draft addressed to randyventures06@gmail.com, containing the promoted app, campaign, selected reason, and any details you choose to add. Your email app sends it only if you choose to send. We receive your email address and the content you send, and use it to investigate and respond. Reports and support correspondence are handled through Gmail and retained as needed for support, investigating inappropriate content, and legal obligations. You can hide an app without sending a report.

Data Retention

We keep account and activity data while your account remains active and as needed for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and service operation. If you delete your account, personal data is removed or anonymized through our account deletion workflow, while limited aggregated review data may remain in non-identifiable form.

Your Choices

  • You can use guest browsing without creating an account.
  • You can reset your password through the app or reset-password flow.
  • You can request account deletion from within app settings.
  • You can revoke location access in device settings and use manual area search instead.

Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, private infrastructure, and access controls designed to protect user data. No service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and limit unnecessary exposure.

Children

Bean Hunt is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version here and revise the date at the top of this page.

Questions about this policy can be sent to randyventures06@gmail.com.
