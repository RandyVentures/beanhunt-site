Frequently asked questions

Is Bean Hunt free?

Yes. Finding cafés, hunts, check-ins, badges, streaks, and your coffee passport are all free. Premium is an optional one-time purchase.

Is Bean Hunt Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time unlock, not a subscription. It adds the “Not been yet” filter, passport insights (your go-to order, favorite café, and coffee day), and unlimited saved shops and drinks.

Do I need an account to use Bean Hunt?

No. You can start as a guest and stamp coffees right away. Create a free account whenever you want to back up your passport.

Can Bean Hunt find laptop-friendly coffee shops?

Yes. The laptop-friendly hunt collects cafés near you that work well for getting work done, alongside hunts for top-rated cafés, hidden gems, cafés open right now, and cafés a short walk away.

What devices does Bean Hunt work on?

Bean Hunt is available for iPhone on the App Store.

How do I get help or delete my account?

You can request account deletion from the app's settings. For anything else, email randyventures06@gmail.com.
